Privacy Policy for Sokoban

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Sokoban is a puzzle game for Huawei Lite Wearables. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information we collect

Sokoban does not collect, transmit, share, or sell personal information. The app does not require an account and does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or other personal data.

Local game data

The app stores your completed levels and best step scores locally on your device so progress can be restored when you reopen the app. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us or to third parties.

Your choices and rights

You can delete the locally stored game progress at any time by clearing Sokoban's app data or uninstalling the app. For more details, see our data subject rights information.

Children's privacy

Sokoban does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the Sokoban GitHub repository. Do not include personal or sensitive information in a public issue.
