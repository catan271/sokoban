Data Subject Rights for Sokoban

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Sokoban does not collect or receive personal information. The only app data is completed-level and best-score progress, which is stored locally on your device.

Access and correction

Your progress is visible in the app through the completed-level count and best-score display. You may change it by replaying levels and setting new best scores.

Deletion

You can erase all locally stored progress by clearing Sokoban's app data in your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

Requests and questions

For questions about these rights, open an issue in the Sokoban GitHub repository. GitHub Issues are public, so do not include personal or sensitive information.
